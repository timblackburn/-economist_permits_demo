Chicago building permits, 1883–1930

Weekly permit lists transcribed from scans of Chicago trade papers: mostly The Economist, with The American Contractor and The Real Estate and Building Journal filling in some weeks (selected periods from 1883 to 1930; not every issue is here yet). Some weeks of 1898–1912 come only from the Chicago History Museum’s index of The American Contractor, which has no scans. Text is machine-read and may contain errors. Streets are shown by today's name, with the name printed at the time beneath when it has changed. Click a community area, a bar, an architect, or a row.
